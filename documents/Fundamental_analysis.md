1. Title
   Fleet Maintenance Scheduling Platform

2. Domain
   Fleet Management and Vehicle Maintenance

3. Users
   • Admin
   • Fleet Manager
   • Mechanic / Maintenance Staff

4. Problem
   Companies managing multiple vehicles often rely on manual
   methods to track vehicle maintenance. This makes it difficult
   to identify upcoming services, overdue maintenance, maintenance
   history and associated costs. Missed maintenance can lead to
   vehicle breakdowns, increased repair costs and operational delays.

5. Proposed Solution
   A web-based platform to manage vehicles, schedule maintenance,
   assign mechanics, track maintenance status, record parts and
   costs, monitor overdue services and maintain maintenance history.

6. Core Entities
   • Users
   • Vehicles
   • Maintenance
   • Maintenance Schedule
   • Mechanics
   • Maintenance Parts
   • Maintenance History

7. Roles & Permissions
   • Admin – Full system management
   • Fleet Manager – Vehicle and maintenance scheduling
   • Mechanic – Assigned maintenance operations

8. Success Criteria
   • Vehicle management
   • Maintenance scheduling
   • Mechanic assignment
   • Upcoming/overdue maintenance tracking
   • Maintenance history
   • Parts and cost tracking
   • Dashboard and reports
   • Automated backend testing

9. Out of Scope
   • GPS tracking
   • IoT sensors
   • Payment system
   • Manufacturer integration
   • Automatic spare-parts ordering

10. Chosen Track
    Python – Django REST Framework
